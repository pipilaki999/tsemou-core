# TSEMOU Trust Principles v1.0

## 1. Neutral Baseline

Every company starts from 5/10.

Absence of negative evidence is not positive evidence.
A high score must be earned through documented positive behaviour.

## 2. Evidence Before Opinion

TSEMOU evaluates documented actions, omissions and impacts.
It does not evaluate ideology, popularity or public relations.

## 3. Responsibility Model

A company may be evaluated not only for what it did, but also for what it failed to do when:
- it had responsibility,
- it had knowledge or repeated warnings,
- it had power to prevent or reduce harm,
- and the omission created or prolonged documented harm.

## 4. Leadership & Influence

Leadership actions can affect company trust when they are connected to documented corporate power, financing, lobbying, policy influence or real-world harm.

TSEMOU does not punish private opinion.
TSEMOU evaluates actions, funding, influence and consequences.

## 5. War & Conflict

Companies and leaders can be evaluated when documented evidence links them to:
- war crimes,
- sanctions violations,
- illegal occupation,
- forced displacement,
- weapons or surveillance supply used for abuse,
- financing actors involved in serious harm,
- or failure to comply with legal and humanitarian duties.

## 6. Positive Trust Must Be Earned

Positive evidence can increase Trust when a company:
- treats employees exceptionally well,
- shares meaningful value with workers,
- invests in environmental restoration,
- protects human rights,
- acts transparently,
- repairs harm,
- supports peace or humanitarian protection with verifiable action.

## 7. High Scores Are Rare

5.0 = neutral / unknown
6.0 = some positive evidence
7.0 = good
8.0 = very strong
9.0 = exceptional global standard
9.8+ = extremely rare

## 8. Ranking

TSEMOU must support rankings:
- best companies,
- worst companies,
- by country,
- by industry,
- by category,
- by environment,
- by workers,
- by human rights,
- by transparency,
- by war/conflict responsibility.

## 9. Human Oversight

Critical evidence, especially war, legal, sanctions, human rights and leadership influence evidence, requires human review before publication or major trust impact.

## 10. No Black Box

Every score must be explainable.
Every major impact must show the evidence, policy, source, confidence and reasoning.
